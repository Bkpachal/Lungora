Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Lungora Guide handles information when you visit en-lungora.vercel.app.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. What this site is

This is an independent informational website about Lungora. We do not sell products, take orders, or process payments. Purchases happen on the seller’s website, which has its own privacy policy.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you send us: if you contact us, we receive what you choose to share, such as your email address and message.

We do not currently run our own advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this page.

Our buttons lead to the seller’s website through an affiliate link. When you click, the affiliate network and seller may use cookies or similar technologies to record the referral so a commission can be attributed. We don’t control those technologies; please read their policies.

4. How we use information

  • To operate, secure, and improve this website
  • To respond to messages you send us
  • To comply with legal obligations

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers such as our host, or when required by law.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may be able to ask what personal information we hold and request correction or deletion. You can also block cookies in your browser. See the Contact page.

7. Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service is completely secure. We keep messages only as long as needed to respond and meet legal requirements.

8. Children

This site is for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
