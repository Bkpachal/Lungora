Safety and Important Considerations

“Natural” doesn’t mean right for everyone. Please read this before you order.

Using Lungora responsibly

  • Follow the label and don’t exceed the stated serving.
  • Check the full ingredient list against any allergies or sensitivities.
  • Turmeric, butcher’s broom, and other botanicals can interact with some medicines, including blood thinners and diabetes or blood-pressure medications.
  • Stop use and contact a clinician if you have an unwanted reaction.

Get medical care for symptoms like these

  • Shortness of breath or wheezing
  • A cough that lasts weeks, or coughing up blood
  • Chest pain or tightness
  • Unexplained weight loss or fatigue

A supplement is not a substitute for evaluation. Call emergency services for severe breathing trouble.
